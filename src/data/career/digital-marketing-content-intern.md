#### Job Description

Learn content creation, digital campaign strategy, and brand storytelling. Gain practical experience developing engaging copy, planning content calendars, exploring social media channels, and analyzing performance metrics.

#### Responsibilities

- Draft engaging copy for blog posts, social channels, and product announcements.
- Assist in planning editorial calendars and organizing marketing initiatives.
- Research basic SEO keywords and learn on-page optimization practices.
- Track engagement metrics and evaluate content performance.
- Collaborate with design and research teams to align messaging with brand tone.

#### What You Will Bring

- Strong written communication skills, creativity, and enthusiasm for digital media.
- Curiosity about social media platforms, audience dynamics, and online trends.
- Willingness to learn analytical tools and iterate content based on data.
- Good organization, self-motivation, and dependability.

#### Skills You Can Develop

- Audience research and digital campaign structuring.
- Search engine optimization (SEO) and content marketing fundamentals.
- Copywriting for digital products and tech audiences.
- Data-driven content performance evaluation and social analytics.

#### Benefits of the Program

- Practical learning experience through structured assignments.
- Skill development through real-world scenarios and hands-on exercises.
- Feedback and progress evaluation where available.
- Portfolio-worthy work samples to demonstrate your capabilities.
- Internship completion certificate upon meeting the program's completion requirements.
