#### Job Description

Explore product strategy, user discovery, and business analysis by researching market trends, evaluating competitive offerings, understanding user pain points, and documenting product concepts.

#### Responsibilities

- Conduct desk research on market dynamics, emerging software solutions, and industry benchmarks.
- Synthesize user feedback and map out customer personas and pain points.
- Draft clear product requirement summaries and feature rationale notes.
- Analyze business models, pricing strategies, and user onboarding journeys.
- Present research findings and recommendations to the team in structured sessions.

#### What You Will Bring

- Inquisitive mindset and passion for tech products and business innovation.
- Good analytical and research capabilities with attention to structured details.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
- Collaborative attitude and enthusiasm to learn product development practices.

#### Skills You Can Develop

- Competitive intelligence and market opportunity mapping.
- Product definition, user problem framing, and PRD drafting.
- Business model analysis and value proposition development.
- Structured communication and executive-level reporting.

- Practical learning experience through structured assignments.
- Skill development through real-world scenarios and hands-on exercises.
- Feedback and progress evaluation where available.
