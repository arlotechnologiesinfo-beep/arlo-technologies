# From Automation to Connected Workflows

Automating repetitive tasks is often the first step toward modernizing business operations. However, when individual automations are implemented without cohesive architecture, organizations end up with fragile, unmonitored scripts that break quietly.

This whitepaper provides founders, product managers, and operations leaders with a practical framework for mapping business processes, designing resilient multi-platform integrations, and measuring real business impact.
